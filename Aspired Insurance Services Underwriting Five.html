How a submission moves

Four steps, the same order, every time.

01
Submission

Client submits risk detail and loss history through the portal or an underwriter direct.

02
Risk assessment

An underwriter reviews exposure, engineering reports, and prior claims against our appetite.

03
Terms issued

Pricing, conditions, and any subjectivities are returned with a firm quote validity period.

04
Bind & issue

Cover binds on our capacity paper; policy documents issue within 48 hours.
